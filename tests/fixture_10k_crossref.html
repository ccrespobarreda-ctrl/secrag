ITEM 1. BUSINESS

We design and sell casual footwear through wholesale and direct-to-consumer channels.

Our products are distributed in more than 85 countries through a network of partners.

We operate company-owned retail locations as well as digital commerce platforms globally.

The company was founded in 2002 and is headquartered in Broomfield, Colorado.

We manage the business through two reportable operating segments organized by brand.

ITEM 1A. Risk Factors

We depend on third-party manufacturers located primarily in Asia for substantially all production.

Disruption at any manufacturing partner could materially affect our ability to meet demand.

Foreign currency movements may reduce reported revenue when local results are translated.

Consumer preferences in footwear shift rapidly and past performance may not continue.

We face significant competition from larger companies with greater financial resources.
